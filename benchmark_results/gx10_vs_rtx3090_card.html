Bandwidth vs Capacity

Two NVIDIA machines, same models, same measurement. For anything that fits in 24 GB the RTX 3090 is 3–5× faster — the gap tracks memory bandwidth. But the GB10 runs a 120B model the 3090 can't even load. Pick by the job, not the spec sheet.

Reach for the RTX 3090

Raw speed on models that fit.

  • Fast small/mid models, 3–34B
  • High-throughput / many requests
  • Image generation (SDXL, FLUX)
  • 3–5× the tokens/sec, ≤ 24 GB

Reach for the GX10 (GB10)

Capacity a 24 GB card can't touch.

  • 70B–120B models, run whole
  • Large context windows
  • Private, compact, low-power
  • The only one that loads them at all
